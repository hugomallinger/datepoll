Planning a weekend in a group chat?

Ask which dates work, without typing every date by hand.

Step 1

Click the calendar next to +

Datepoll adds this button to every chat on WhatsApp Web.

Step 2

Pick the days

Click days, or shift-click for a range. With Group by week, each weekend is one option.

Step 3

Create the poll

Datepoll fills in WhatsApp's own poll dialog.

Step 4

You press Send.

You check the poll and send it yourself. Nothing is sent for you.

Everyone votes.

The results show up in the chat as people vote.
