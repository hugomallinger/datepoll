# Datepoll privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Datepoll is a Chrome extension that helps you create date polls on WhatsApp Web. This page explains what it does with your data.

## Summary

Datepoll does not collect, send or sell any data. It has no server, no analytics and no tracking.

## What Datepoll accesses

- **WhatsApp Web (`web.whatsapp.com`):** Datepoll adds a button to the chat footer. When you click **Create poll**, it opens WhatsApp's poll dialog and types in the question and date options you chose. It does not read your messages, contacts or chat list, and it never sends a message or poll. You press Send yourself.

## What Datepoll stores

Datepoll saves your panel settings with Chrome's storage (`chrome.storage.sync`):

- your time slots (for example 18:00)
- the day filter (all days, weekdays, weekends)
- the label language
- your own poll question, if you changed the default one
- "Group by week" and "Allow multiple answers"

If you're signed in to Chrome with sync turned on, Chrome syncs these settings between your own browsers, as it does for other extension settings. Datepoll's developer can't see them. Removing the extension deletes them.

Selected dates are not saved.

## Third parties

Datepoll shares no data with anyone and loads no code or content from outside the extension.
